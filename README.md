# Dingus

See the XP of your friends, party members and guildmates in a small, quiet panel, and find out when they level up.

Dingus only shows people who also run it, so install it together with the people you level with.

## What you see

Each person gets one bar in their class colour, with their name, level and XP inside it. The bar also marks their rested XP and the XP from quests they've completed but not yet turned in.

Hover a bar to see:

- Exact XP, rested XP and completed-quest XP
- XP per hour and the time to the next level
- Their zone, if they share it
- How many levels ahead of or behind you they are
- Their other characters

## Who's listed

People are grouped into Party, Friends and Guild, and each person appears only once. Sections can be collapsed, and empty ones stay hidden.

Friends and party members are listed by default. In the settings you can also list guildmates, offline players, max-level players and your own bar.

The panel stays empty until someone else running Dingus is listed.

## Using the panel

- **Move and resize:** drag the panel to move it, and hover it and drag its corner or edges to resize it. You can lock both in the settings.
- **Hide or open settings:** hover the panel for a header with Hide/Show and Options.
- **Right-click a bar:** Whisper, Invite, Pin to top, Hide, or stop sharing your XP with that person.

## Level-up alerts

Dingus can show a toast, play a sound, or do both when someone levels up. By default it does both, for friends only. You can change this to everyone, pinned players only, or turn alerts off.

## Settings

Type `/dingus` to open the settings. Among other things, you can choose:

- What the bar text shows, and normal or compact rows
- A details line under each bar, with zone, XP per hour and time to level
- How the list is sorted: by level, progress, name or recent activity
- When the panel shows: always, out of combat, out of instances, out of both, or never
- Whether the panel fades until you hover it

## What you share

Dingus sends your character's name, class, level, XP and rested XP, the XP from your completed quests, and your zone to other players running it. By default it shares with friends, guild and party.

You can turn off sharing with each of those groups, leave out your zone, or stop sharing with one person from the bar's right-click menu.

Guild and party messages reach everyone in that guild or party who runs Dingus. That includes anyone you stopped sharing with one by one, so turn off Guild or Party sharing if that matters to you.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/dingus` | Open the settings |
| `/dingus reset` | Move the panel back to its starting position |
